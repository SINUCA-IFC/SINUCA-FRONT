This derived catalog retains ISO alpha-2 codes and Portuguese common/official
translations for all 250 entries (countries and territories). The database in
countries.json is available under ODbL 1.0. It is not a statement about recognition
or sovereignty. Refresh the snapshot when country names change.

Flag image URLs use https://flagcdn.com/ (Flagpedia).
Images are downloaded on registration and uploaded through the existing image
endpoint so the registered delegation retains its own flag in the backend.
Searching names works locally; loading flag images requires internet access.
